KEY ATTRACTIONS

Explore the beauty and culture of Brunei through its stunning locations and attractions.

Ulu Temburong National Park

Ulu Temburong National Park is a pristine rainforest located in the Temburong District of Brunei. It is known for its rich biodiversity, lush greenery, and unique wildlife. Visitors can explore the park through guided tours, canopy walks, and river cruises, making it a must-visit destination for nature enthusiasts.

Masjid Omar Ali Safuddien

Masjid Omar Ali Safuddien is the largest mosque in Brunei and one of the most iconic landmarks in the country. It is known for its stunning architecture, intricate details, and spiritual significance. The mosque can accommodate up to 10,000 worshippers and is a popular destination for both locals and tourists.

Kampong Ayer

Kampong Ayer is a traditional floating village located in the Brunei River. It is known for its unique architecture, with houses built on stilts over the water. The village offers a glimpse into the traditional way of life in Brunei, with its vibrant markets, colorful boats, and friendly locals.

Jerudong Park

Jerudong Park is a popular amusement park located in the Brunei-Muara District. It is known for its thrilling rides, family-friendly attractions, and beautiful gardens. The park offers a fun-filled experience for visitors of all ages, making it a great destination for families and adventure seekers.
