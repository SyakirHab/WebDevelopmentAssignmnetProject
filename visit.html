Plan Your Visit to Brunei

Brunei is a small country located on the island of Borneo in Southeast Asia. It is known for its rich culture, beautiful landscapes, and friendly people. Visitors can explore the capital city of Bandar Seri Begawan, visit the stunning mosques and palaces, and enjoy the natural beauty of the rainforests and beaches. Brunei is also famous for its delicious cuisine, which includes dishes such as nasi lemak, satay, and beef rendang. Whether you're interested in history, nature, or food, Brunei has something to offer for everyone.

Travel Tips

  • Best time to visit: March to October
  • Currency: Brunei Dollar (BND)
  • Language: Malay (official), English widely spoken
  • Visa requirements: Here
  • Local customs: Dress modestly, especially when visiting religious sites
  • Friday all-day closures for religious observance
