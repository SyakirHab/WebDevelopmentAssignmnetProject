Plan Your Visit to Brunei

Brunei is a small country on the island of Borneo in Southeast Asia. Known for its rich culture, beautiful landscapes, and friendly people, it offers stunning mosques and palaces, rainforests, beaches, and delicious cuisine — nasi lemak, satay, and beef rendang. Whether you're into history, nature, or food, Brunei has something for everyone.

Travel Tips

  • 👗 Dress modestly, especially at religious sites.
  • 🕌 Friday all-day closures for religious observance.
  • 🍽️ Try local dishes: nasi lemak, ambuyat, satay.
  • 🚗 Use the Dart app for public transport.
  • 💰 Cards accepted in cities; carry cash for markets.
